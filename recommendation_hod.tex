\noindent \textbf{Subject:} Recommendation and Endorsement for Financial Grant to the Student Agritech Startup.

\vspace{1.5em}

\noindent This is to strongly recommend and endorse the financial grant proposal submitted by Goparaju Karthik, a B.Tech 4th-year student of the Artificial Intelligence \& Data Science branch (Batch 2023--2027, Regd. No. 248w5a5404), for his startup project in the Agritech domain. 

\vspace{1em}
\noindent The recommendation is based on the following key evaluation points:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Multidisciplinary Innovation:} The startup operates at the core intersection of Agriculture, Mechanical, Automobile Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence, demonstrating a unique technical approach to solving modern agricultural challenges.
    \item \textbf{Socio-Economic Rural Impact:} The proposed prototype utilizes affordable mechanization and AI-driven automation, which directly addresses labor shortages and operational inefficiencies in rural agricultural sectors, positively impacting small-scale farmers.
    \item \textbf{Financial Justification:} The requested grant of Rs. 1,25,000/- is highly reasonable and strictly budgeted, allocating Rs. 1,00,000/- for prototype development and Rs. 25,000/- for Pvt. Ltd. company registration and incorporation.
    \item \textbf{High Growth Potential:} The venture has demonstrated high potential to scale. Supporting this project at this juncture will facilitate a smooth transition from a student project into a legally registered corporate entity.
    \item \textbf{Institutional Alignment:} Fostering such high-potential student-led entrepreneurship aligns with the core vision of our institution. Providing this financial backing will set a benchmark for cultivating startup culture in the department.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent Hence, the proposal is strongly recommended for the sanction of the suggested grant.
